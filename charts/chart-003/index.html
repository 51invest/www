    数据来源与许可

    • Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED) · FRED series UNRATE, retrieved from the CSV endpoint · 来源页
      许可: FRED legal page, verbatim: "These series may be under copyright or in the public domain and may be used without permission, provided you do not engage in any prohibited use." and "When using, please cite the data source and acknowledge that you obtained the data from FRED".
      定义: The unemployment rate represents the number of unemployed as a percentage of the labor force.
    • Bank for International Settlements · Consumer prices statistics (long series), monthly annual rate of change · 来源页
      许可: BIS statistics terms of permitted use; the BIS must be cited as the source. Source: Bank for International Settlements.
      定义: Year-on-year change of the BIS-compiled U.S. consumer price index (2010 = 100), in percent. Months the flow could not compute carry OBS_STATUS M or another non-actual status and are skipped.
    派生说明
    • US_UNEMPLOYMENT: Source monthly observation, no interpolation.
    • US_CPI: Source monthly observation with OBS_STATUS A, no interpolation.

    Monthly unemployment rate (percent, seasonally adjusted) paired with the BIS-compiled U.S. CPI annual rate 2024-01 to 2026-09; 32 observations for the unemployment series: 2025-10 is blank, the same month the BIS CPI flow carries no value, so the two series pair on these months. The two series are both written as per cent but measure different things (a rate level versus a year-on-year change), so no shared vertical axis is implied here: the layout ruling for this chart is still to be made and designRuling/researchReview are not yet filled.     研究复核

    AI-only 复核 · Doubao (AI) · 2026-11-22 · 结论: passed

    这张图把美国失业率和 CPI 同比分成上下两个面板，用来看两个劳动市场与物价指标在同一段时间里怎么走。它不画菲利普斯曲线，也不宣称两者存在稳定替代关系。

    • 数据与来源：失业率来自 FRED 的 UNRATE（原始编制方为美国劳工统计局，季调），CPI 来自 BIS 月度同比；32 个月，2024-01 至 2026-09，2025-10 因 BIS 缺值整月剔除。失业率 3.7%–4.5%，CPI 2.31%–4.25%。
    • 方法与口径：两条序列都按月度共同样本取数，缺月整月剔除；没有插值。32 个月不足以估计稳定的菲利普斯曲线关系，页面因此只做并列展示，不计算斜率或均衡失业率。
    • 图形与无障碍：失业率与通胀单位不同，采用上下分面，各自有纵轴；面板之间只比较走势，不比较水平。图注与数据表保留这一说明。
    • 英文表述：英文页把“unemployment rate”和“CPI annual rate”分开命名，没有把失业率写成“jobless claims”。

    残余风险与不适用范围

    • 32 个月样本短，不能据此推断菲利普斯曲线的斜率或稳定性。
    • 失业率与 CPI 的定义、季调与覆盖不同，分面只能看方向，不能看水平差。
