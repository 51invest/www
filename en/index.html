Read the economy.
Think through investing.

Why a price moves, why a loan gets approved, which line of a statement matters. These questions can be taken apart. The main site raises them with charts; six stations explain the mechanism, the definition and the boundary.

How to read the site Knowledge stations Content map What is here

Where to start: Each route runs four to six articles, with reading time and section count on every entry. Finish one before starting the next. The 276 articles total about 30 hr 9 min; there is no need to read them in order.

Where to start

Two lines connect the six stations: one runs from understanding the economy to investment judgement; the other uses history and research to discipline time and evidence.

Main line

From reading the economy to making a judgement

Start with choice, markets and accounts; then see how money moves across time and what a product actually contains; finally put goals, constraints and risk into one portfolio. The four stations share a language, so you do not relearn the same terms at every stop.

Why do prices, growth and policy change the way they do? Economics → How does money travel across time and risk? Finance → What can be invested in, and what is inside each product? Markets → How does a judgement become an executable portfolio? Investing
Cross-cutting line

Put every judgement back into time and evidence

History shows that institutions change and that the same mechanism can produce different outcomes in different eras. Research shows how strong a conclusion the data can support. Neither station supplies answers; both supply constraints that keep market and investment claims honest.

Where did today’s institutions and theories come from? History → How strong a conclusion does the data support? Research → How does a judgement become an executable portfolio? Investing

Six stations, each with its own questions

A price, a loan, a financial statement, a portfolio: first find the station that owns it, then go deeper. Every station has a brief, four common misreadings, six key terms and its full article catalogue.
