    Derivation
    • EUR_USD: Arithmetic mean of ECB daily reference rates in the month.
    • US_EFFECTIVE_FED_FUNDS: Arithmetic mean of NY Fed daily EFFR observations in the month.
    • EA_DEPOSIT_RATE: Last available daily policy-rate observation in the month.
    • RATE_DIFFERENTIAL: US_EFFECTIVE_FED_FUNDS minus EA_DEPOSIT_RATE. EFFR is a market rate and DFR is a policy rate; the difference is not an arbitrage spread.

    Research review

    AI-only review · Doubao (AI) · 2026-11-22 · Verdict: passed

    This chart places EUR/USD and the U.S.–euro-area rate differential in two panels. The differential is EFFR minus the ECB deposit facility rate — a market rate minus a policy rate — and the page states it is not an arbitrage spread.

    • Data and sources：EUR/USD is the ECB daily reference rate, EFFR is from the NY Fed, and the deposit facility rate is from the ECB. 33 months, 2024-01 to 2026-09, zero missing. EUR/USD ranges 1.0354–1.1824; the differential ranges 1.249–2.33 percentage points.
    • Method and definitions：The exchange rate and EFFR are monthly averages; the deposit rate is the last available observation in the month, and the differential formula is recorded. EUR/USD correlations with EFFR, the deposit rate and the differential are −0.695, −0.773 and 0.479; the page does not turn them into trading signals.
    • Chart design and accessibility：The exchange rate (USD/EUR) and the differential (percentage points) use different units, so the chart uses panels and no dual axis. The derivation states that EFFR is a market rate and DFR a policy rate, so the difference is not an arbitrage spread.
    • English wording：The English page keeps the precise names “reference rate”, “effective federal funds rate” and “deposit facility rate”; the differential is not called “carry”.

    Residual risks and scope limits

    • The 33-month sample is short and correlations are window-dependent; the page does not estimate an exchange-rate model.
    • The differential is not an arbitrage spread and cannot be used to derive forward rates or a trading strategy.
