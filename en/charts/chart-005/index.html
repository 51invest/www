    Research review

    AI-only review · Doubao (AI) · 2026-11-22 · Verdict: passed

    This chart places the nominal gold price and the 10-year U.S. Treasury real yield in two panels to compare their direction over the same period. It does not claim they must move inversely, nor treat a 33-month correlation as a pricing model.

    • Data and sources：Gold is the World Bank Pink Sheet monthly price in USD per troy ounce; the real yield is the U.S. Treasury 10-year daily real yield averaged by month. 33 months, 2024-01 to 2026-09, zero missing. Gold ranges 2,023–5,020 USD/oz; the real yield ranges 1.62%–2.64%.
    • Method and definitions：The real yield is a monthly average of available daily observations, dropping months with fewer than ten daily observations; gold uses the monthly observation directly, with no interpolation. The full-window correlation is only +0.15, so the page reports co-movement in the same period and does not claim that a rising real yield must suppress gold.
    • Chart design and accessibility：Gold (USD/oz) and the real yield (percent per annum) use different units, so the chart uses panels with separate axes; panels compare movement only. The table keeps source units.
    • English wording：The English page distinguishes the “nominal gold price” from the “10-year real yield”; the real yield is not mislabelled as nominal.

    Residual risks and scope limits

    • The 33-month window is short; the +0.15 correlation is weak and window-dependent, and the page offers no gold pricing model.
    • The real yield is a monthly average while gold is a monthly quote; their timing is not identical.
