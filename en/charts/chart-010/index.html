    Research review

    AI-only review · Doubao (AI) · 2026-11-22 · Verdict: passed

    This chart puts inflation rates for the United States, Japan, China, the euro area and the United Kingdom on one axis to compare levels and co-movement since 2015. National CPI/HICP baskets and definitions differ, so the page compares published national annual rates and makes no “who has higher inflation” ranking.

    • Data and sources：The U.S., Japan, China and the UK come from the BIS long series monthly annual rate; the euro area from the ECB HICP annual rate. 140 months, 2015-01 to 2026-09; 2025-10 is excluded from the common sample. Ranges: U.S. −0.20%–9.06%, Japan −1.12%–4.35%, China −0.80%–5.62%, euro area −0.6%–10.6%, UK −0.20%–11.09%.
    • Method and definitions：All five series are aligned on a common monthly sample and missing months are dropped. BIS and ECB annual-rate definitions are recorded separately. Full-window correlations are 0.90 for U.S.~euro area and 0.97 for euro area~UK, but this is co-movement under common shocks, not institutional causation.
    • Chart design and accessibility：All five are annual-rate percentages, so one axis is valid; baskets differ, and sources, definitions and captions preserve that. No dual axis is used.
    • English wording：The English page distinguishes CPI from HICP and states that the UK uses the BIS compilation while the euro area uses ECB HICP; the five lines are not presented as one harmonised index.

    Residual risks and scope limits

    • National CPI/HICP baskets, weights and adjustments differ; levels are not a direct cost-of-living comparison.
    • The 2025-10 gap and the 2026-02 HICP dataflow change leave breaks; the page does not estimate structural breaks.
