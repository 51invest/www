    Research review

    AI-only review · Doubao (AI) · 2026-11-22 · Verdict: passed

    This chart rebases U.S. industrial production and the World Bank silver price to January 2024 = 100 to compare their movement. It does not claim silver prices are driven only by industrial demand, nor treat the 0.69 correlation as causation.

    • Data and sources：Industrial production is the Federal Reserve G.17 total index (2017=100, seasonally adjusted); silver is the World Bank Pink Sheet monthly price in USD per troy ounce. 33 months, 2024-01 to 2026-09, zero missing. Silver ranges 22.7–92.1 USD/oz; the indexed series peaks at 402.18.
    • Method and definitions：Both series are divided by their own 2024-01 value and multiplied by 100; the derivation is recorded. The full-window correlation is 0.693. Silver also carries investment, safe-haven and monetary roles, so the page does not present industrial demand as the sole driver.
    • Chart design and accessibility：After indexing, both series share a 2024-01 = 100 basis, so one axis is valid; source units remain in the labels and table.
    • English wording：The English page uses “industrial production” and “silver”, with the same indexing basis as the Chinese page.

    Residual risks and scope limits

    • Only 33 months: the 0.693 correlation is window-dependent, and the page does not estimate a silver supply-demand model.
    • Indexing preserves relative movement only; absolute USD/oz prices require the data table.
