    Research review

    AI-only review · Doubao (AI) · 2026-11-22 · Verdict: passed

    This chart places the monthly WTI price and U.S. commercial crude stocks in two panels to compare their movement over the same period. Stocks use the last weekly observation inside each month, with no averaging or interpolation; the page does not claim stocks alone determine prices.

    • Data and sources：WTI is the World Bank Pink Sheet monthly price in USD per barrel; stocks are EIA WCESTUS1 (thousand barrels, all U.S. locations). 33 months, 2024-01 to 2026-09, zero missing. WTI ranges 57.9–99.1 USD/bbl; stocks range 406,987–461,636 thousand barrels.
    • Method and definitions：Stocks take the last weekly value whose week-end date falls inside the calendar month, and the rule is recorded; the level correlation is 0.406 and the month-change correlation 0.455, both descriptive. The page does not import seasonal maintenance, strategic reserves or refinery runs, so it makes no causal interpretation.
    • Chart design and accessibility：Price (USD/bbl) and stocks (thousand barrels) use different units, so the chart uses panels with separate axes; the two are never put on one axis. The table keeps 33 monthly rows.
    • English wording：The English page uses “commercial crude oil stocks” and “WTI” to match the Chinese page; thousand barrels are not mislabelled as million barrels.

    Residual risks and scope limits

    • 33 months of month-end stocks cannot identify weekly or seasonal causal chains.
    • Both stocks and prices are exposed to refinery runs, exports and strategic reserve releases that the page does not show.
