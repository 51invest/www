    Research review

    AI-only review · Doubao (AI) · 2026-11-22 · Verdict: passed

    This chart pairs euro-area HICP annual inflation with the ECB deposit facility rate to compare their movement. Both are percentages, but one is an annual rate of change and the other a policy-rate level, so only direction is comparable.

    • Data and sources：HICP comes from the ECB HICP dataflow (M.U2.N.000000.4D0.ANR); the deposit facility rate from the ECB FM dataflow. 33 months, 2024-01 to 2026-09, zero missing. HICP ranges 1.7%–3.8%, the deposit rate 2.0%–4.0%.
    • Method and definitions：HICP is the actual monthly annual-rate observation (OBS_STATUS A); the deposit rate is the last available observation in the month. Both align by month with no interpolation. The full-window correlation is 0.65, and the page reports co-movement only, not that rates determine inflation.
    • Chart design and accessibility：Both are per-year percentages and share one axis; the page notes “annual rate vs policy-rate level”. The definition records the 2026-02-04 dataflow change from ICP to HICP.
    • English wording：The English page keeps “HICP annual rate” and “deposit facility rate”; the deposit rate is not presented as an unqualified “policy rate”.

    Residual risks and scope limits

    • The 33-month sample is short and the 0.65 correlation is window-dependent; the page does not estimate policy lags.
    • The HICP dataflow change and the within-month timing of the deposit rate leave definitional breaks.
