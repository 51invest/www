    Research review

    AI-only review · Doubao (AI) · 2026-11-22 · Verdict: passed

    This chart rebases maize and WTI crude to January 2024 = 100 and puts them on one axis to compare relative movement. The full-window correlation is only 0.29, so the page does not claim a stable cost-pass-through.

    • Data and sources：Maize and WTI both come from the same World Bank Pink Sheet monthly table. 33 months, 2024-01 to 2026-09, zero missing. Maize ranges 170.3–239.6 USD/t, WTI 57.9–99.1 USD/bbl; the indexed series range 78.35–134.10.
    • Method and definitions：Both series divide by their own 2024-01 value and multiply by 100; the derivation is recorded. The full-window correlation is 0.29, a weak relationship. The page does not present biofuel, fertiliser or freight cost chains as conclusions; it shows price movement only.
    • Chart design and accessibility：After indexing, both series share a 2024-01 = 100 basis and one axis is valid; source units and absolute prices remain in the table.
    • English wording：The English page uses “maize” and “WTI crude oil”, matching the Chinese page, without using “corn” and “maize” for different series.

    Residual risks and scope limits

    • Only 33 months: the weak 0.29 correlation is window-dependent, and the page does not estimate a cost-pass-through model.
    • Indexing preserves relative movement only; absolute USD/t and USD/bbl prices require the data table.
