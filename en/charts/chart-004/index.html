    Research review

    AI-only review · Doubao (AI) · 2026-11-22 · Verdict: passed

    This chart rebases U.S. industrial production and the World Bank copper price to January 2024 = 100 and puts them on one axis to compare relative movement. It does not claim that industrial production alone determines copper prices, nor treat the 0.85 correlation as causation.

    • Data and sources：Industrial production is the Federal Reserve G.17 total index (2017=100, seasonally adjusted); copper is the World Bank Pink Sheet monthly price in USD per metric ton. 33 months, 2024-01 to 2026-09, zero missing. Raw production ranges 99.22–103.95; copper ranges 8,305–14,474 USD/t.
    • Method and definitions：Both series are divided by their own 2024-01 value and multiplied by 100; the derivation is itemised in the page’s derivation note. The full-window level correlation is 0.852. Copper also responds to mine supply, inventories, the dollar and investment demand; the page does not turn correlation into a single cause.
    • Chart design and accessibility：After indexing, both series are on a 2024-01 = 100 basis, so one axis is valid; original units remain in the unit labels and data table. No dual axis is used.
    • English wording：The English page maps “industrial production” and “copper” one-to-one with the Chinese page and keeps the indexing note.

    Residual risks and scope limits

    • Only 33 months: the 0.852 correlation changes with the window, and the page does not estimate a copper supply-demand model.
    • Indexing preserves relative movement only; absolute USD/t prices are not readable from the line itself.
