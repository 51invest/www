    Research review

    AI-only review · Doubao (AI) · 2026-11-22 · Verdict: passed

    This chart rebases gold and silver to January 2024 = 100 and adds the gold/silver ratio in ounces of silver per ounce of gold. It shows relative movement and does not treat the 0.95 correlation as proof that relative value must work.

    • Data and sources：Gold and silver both come from the same World Bank Pink Sheet monthly table in USD per troy ounce. 33 months, 2024-01 to 2026-09, zero missing. Gold ranges 2,023–5,020 USD/oz, silver 22.7–92.1 USD/oz, and the ratio 51.61–100.88.
    • Method and definitions：Both indices divide by their own 2024-01 value and multiply by 100; the ratio is same-month gold divided by silver. The full-window correlation is 0.949, showing strong co-movement, but high correlation does not prove the ratio mean-reverts; the page makes no relative-value trade recommendation.
    • Chart design and accessibility：The gold and silver indices share a 2024-01 = 100 basis; the ratio is in ounces of silver per ounce of gold and is kept separate from the indices rather than mixed on one axis. The table keeps raw prices and the ratio.
    • English wording：The English page uses “gold/silver ratio” with its unit and does not describe it as relative strength or a trading signal.

    Residual risks and scope limits

    • The 33-month sample is short and the 0.949 correlation is window-dependent; the page does not prove ratio mean reversion.
    • The ratio reflects investment and industrial demand in both markets and is not a stand-alone relative-value test.
