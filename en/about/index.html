ABOUT · 51INVEST

A learning site, not a stock-tipping site

51invest raises questions with charts; six stations explain the mechanism, the measure and the boundary. Everything is free to read. No courses are sold and no investment advice is given.

Who runs it

The site is built and maintained by the 51invest maintainers. Articles are drafted with AI assistance and go through two AI editorial passes; the record sits in the “AI review record” on each article.

There is no human sign-off. AI review can check structure, sources, figures and bilingual consistency, but it cannot replace subject experts, translation review, legal review or data-licence review.

Why it exists

Many economic and investment questions are not short of conclusions; they are short of definitions, measures and boundaries. The site starts with a price, a loan or a line in a statement, then sets out the definitions, mechanisms and sources that can be checked.

It does not forecast prices, give buy or sell advice, or present teaching diagrams as real data. When you see a number, check its date, unit, sample and source first.

What is here

The main site carries charts, courses, the glossary and the full content map. Six stations — economics, finance, markets, invest, history and research — carry the cross-referenced knowledge articles.

  • 351 bilingual articles across 107 knowledge modules
  • 12 data charts with sources and snapshot dates
  • 6 courses and 68 glossary terms
  • At least four sources and ten exercises per article, with review records and stated limits

How it is funded

The site is a non-commercial free publication. Readers are not charged, there are no ads and there are no paid subscriptions. Hosting uses GitHub Pages; third-party data and documents remain under their own licences and are not relicensed here.

How to reach us

If a fact, figure, unit, date, translation, source licence or accessibility issue looks wrong, open an issue in the public deployment repository. Include the page URL, the passage, the evidence you found and the sentence you want checked.
